So we solved it and made an empty pension. Easy enough. But there was a bigger problem.

Seccl and Gaudi were not in the business of making things faster as we were. And they were two of the essential parts to making a pension.

And whilst HSBC was sensible enough to automate account creation, Seccl and Gaudi did it the way their forefathers did: slowly and carefully, by hand. The true craftsmen that they were.

So again, I had to figure a way to crack the stalemate. And crack I did.

Upon studying and probing their process, it was made clear to me that Gaudi really did need an ID, after Seccl got their ID. And HSBC really did need an ID, after Gaudi. And we sure needed the HSBC one to make the pension, as they were the crucial link in holding the money.

So we set out to help them by advancing IDs.
