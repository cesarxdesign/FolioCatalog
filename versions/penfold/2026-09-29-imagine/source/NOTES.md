## The 33 steps

Under the sketches: the first onboarding as one straight row of 33 live screens, scrolled sideways. Built by
`source/flow33.py` from the CodeCatalog desktop screens, each put into the state its step shows. Order and
states follow the flow picture (`Portfolio/penfold-air/img/onboarding-mvp.webp`). The bar under each step is
the colour of its square on that picture, the modules the flow was later split into.

Not in CodeCatalog, drawn in `flow33.py` from the picture: the assumptions dialog (13), every doc accepted (26),
the checks screen (31). Montserrat and Roboto Mono are in `site/_vendor/` for these screens.
