## Order, and why

1. **6x signups** - the flip to existing pension holders. The biggest result, business-wide.
   Needs one line of context (the product already worked and still sat at 100 a month).
2. **7 days** - proof of concept to live, and the funding round closed on it.
3. **Instant** - five days of partner hand-offs to a pension on the spot. Self-contained.
4. **Half the engineering load** - account first, five modules, one component set. Needs one
   line of context (the week-one 33-step flow).
5. **1 day** - the feedback loop, and Joe T.'s beneficiary screen as its proof.

Each section's tag is its metric, so the left column reads as the ranking. Context a win needs is
kept to a line, in a "Context" strip, instead of making the page chronological.
